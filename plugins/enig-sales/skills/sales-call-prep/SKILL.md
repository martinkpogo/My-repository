# sales-call-prep

Identify the purpose and context of the call. Prepare a call using only evidence and source material actually provided or legitimately accessible. Distinguish verified facts from client-reported claims, assumptions, hypotheses, and unresolved questions. Preserve traceable source references. Identify gaps and useful questions. Avoid inventing client history, qualification outcomes, investment ranges, approvals, or record state. Make no governed writes and take no external action.
